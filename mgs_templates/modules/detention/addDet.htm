Issuing a detention:

  • Detentions can only be cancelled by the School Steward once they have been submitted.
  • Green Detentions: The detention will automatically be confirmed and an email notification will be sent out to the offending student with the details of the assigned detention.
  • Yellow Detentions: An email notification will be sent to the School Steward. Upon the School Steward confirming this detention, an email notification will be sent to out to the offending student with the details of the assigned detention.
  • Red Detentions: An email notification will be sent out to the relevant Head of House. Upon the Head of House talking to the offending student and his parents then it is up to the Head of House to confirm this detention. Upon confirmation, an email notification will be sent out to the offending student with the details of the assigned detention.
