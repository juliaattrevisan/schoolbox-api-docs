Issuing a detention:

  • Detentions can only be cancelled by the School Steward once they have been submitted.
  • Please record any additional reasons for the detention in the Details field.
  • Green Detentions: The detention will automatically be confirmed and an email notification will be sent out to the offending student with the details of the assigned detention.
  • Yellow Detentions: An email notification will be sent to the School Steward. Upon the School Steward confirming this detention, an email notification will be sent to out to the offending student with the details of the assigned detention.
  • Red Detentions: An email notification will be sent out to the relevant Head of House. Upon the Head of House talking to the offending student and his parents then it is up to the Head of House to confirm this detention. Upon confirmation, an email notification will be sent out to the offending student with the details of the assigned detention.

Modifying a detention:

  • Change in Type: Only change the type of the detention if it was entered incorrectly in the first place OR if it has not been confirmed as yet and a new type has been decided upon by the School Steward or Head of House. Please note the reason for this change in the Details section. NOTE: A change in type will affect the date for this detention. Make sure a new date is chosen upon changing the detention type.
  • Upgrading: Do this when there are multiple detentions issued to one student and you want to upgrade the detentions to a stronger single detention OR when a student did not show up to an assigned detention class without a valid excuse. Enter the reason for the upgrade into the Details section then click on the Upgrade button in the top right hand corner of the form.
    In the case of combining multiple detentions upgrade each of the detentions for that student, upon upgrading each detention then create a new detention that combines the punishment. In the case of a No-show upgrade, just upgrade this particular detention then create a new one with 'No Show Penalty' as the new offence. NOTE: If the student had a valid excuse for not showing up to his detention, simply change the date for this detention...see below for more detail. 'No-show' will be recorded in the history for this detention but will not be included in Reports under 'No-show' status. Detentions with a status of 'No-show' in the Reporting feature refer to those students who did not show up to their detention and did not have a valid excuse.
  • Change in offence: Do this if the original offence was incorrectly chosen OR if you are upgrading a detention. See above.
  • Change in Date: Do this as long as the detention is still active or in the case of a 'No-show' detention where the student has a valid excuse for not turning up. NOTE: Do not change the Offence in the case of a "No-show" if the student had a valid excuse.
