# Distributed Teams

As the Product Team has grown, there is an increasing number of members who for various reasons are unable to work in
the standard office environment.

> This may be either on a short or long term basis: a team member may need to work from home for a day to take care of
> family, or may be permanently based in a location far away from the office.

For that reason, it is important to consider how to be inclusive of Product Team members who are not in the office.
Commonly, these team members are called "Remote Workers": we prefer the term "Distributed Teams" (DT) as it doesn't
imply as strongly that these team members are Remote from the team that is on-premise.


## The responsibilities of others towards Distributed Team Members

### Keep them in the loop

Please include DT members in conversation (beyond their duties in [Ceremonies](ceremonies.md)), and up to date with
office happenings.
> There's an inevitable disconnect that results from not being face-to-face with the rest of the team: this may help
> them to feel more of a part of the overall team.

### Facilitate participation

If you are organising a meeting in which a DT member will participate in, it's up to you to ensure that it runs
smoothly for them. Ensure that whatever the DT member needs in order to participate is set up in advance; this includes
but isn't limited to:
* the meeting room
* a laptop with a GotoMeeting (or other remote meeting software) meeting ready to go

In larger meetings, a proxy may be assigned to a DT member: the Distributed Team member will private message the proxy
when they wish to speak, and the proxy will then notify the in-person attendees so that the DT member may speak without
accidental interruption.
> This is intended to reduce the problem of DT members not being aware of subtle cues in in-person conversation that
> someone is done speaking.


## Your responsibility as a Distributed Team Member

### You are still a full member of the Product Team

You are still expected to participate in all relevant [Ceremonies](ceremonies.md). Another Team member will
[facilitate your participation](#facilitate-participation) in them.

Remember that you are still valued as a member of the team: whilst you may not be able to attend the office in person on
a regular basis, you are always welcome to visit and partake in social occasions.
