# Workshops

All workshops should include stakeholders and relevant SMEs.

Workshops have been classified under one of the four stages in the double diamond.

## Discovery Workshops

### Problem Statement Refinement
Pre-reading: “What’s Your Problem?” by Matt Lavoie ([Part 1](https://drive.google.com/file/d/0B3NUH_c2zEemOTdveGRXSEFwaFk/view), [Part 2](https://drive.google.com/open?id=0B3NUH_c2zEemZzdGZ2hGYVhZNUk))

    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    Problem space is determined
    Prepared by:    Product Owner
    Deliverables:   Problem statement

### Questions and Assumptions Session
    Involves:       Whole team
    Preparation:    Epics written
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Questions
                    Assumptions
                    Constraints

### Research Planning
    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    None
    Deliverables:   What kind of research is needed?
                    Put the content together
       
             
## Definition Workshops

### Persona Refinement
    Involves:       Individual
    Preparation:    Prior research
    Deliverables:   Persona profile

### Affinity Mapping Session
    Involves:       Whole team 
    Preparation:    post-its are written from the insights gathered
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Completed affinity map

### Journey Mapping
    Involves:       Individual
    Preparation:    None
    Deliverables:   Completed journey map

### Story Writing
    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    Any previous “Define” sessions complete
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Stories ready for “Design” phase


## Design Workshops

### 6-up:1-up
    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    Reviewing “Define” artifacts
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Rough sketches, ranked according to perceived likelihood of success

### Wireframing Session
    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    Checking over brainstorming
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Refined lo-fidelity sketches, in digital form

### Feasibility Testing
    Involves:       Individual
    Preparation:    Wireframes developed
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   None (just a quick and simple analysis of whether the wireframe in question is feasible)

### User Flow Diagrams / Storyboarding
    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    Review sketches
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Completed storyboards for necessary personas and scenarios


## Develop Workshops

### Prototype Construction
    Involves:       Individual
    Preparation:    Review “Design” artefacts
    Prepared by:    Individual

### Usability Testing
    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    Solicit end-users (use TAMs for community engagement)
    Prepared by:    Strike Team

### Task Writing
    Involves:       Strike Team
    Preparation:    Prototypes ratified by usability testing
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Tasks written in a preliminary manner

### Task Refinement
    Involves:       Whole team
    Preparation:    Tasks written and curated
    Prepared by:    Strike Team
    Deliverables:   Tasks made ready for sprint
