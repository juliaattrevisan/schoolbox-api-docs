# Logging Time

## Software
Most of the developers use [Toggl](https://toggl.com).

## Function Point
All your time at work should logged on [Function Point](https://functionpoint.com/). On Function Point you just need to separate your time spent working on the current major release, or the upcoming major release. Use job `#2911: AL: House` for any time that's not spent coding.

## Sprint Actuals
After the Sprint is complete - we log our time in a spreadsheet separated by Dev (Sprint work), BAU, Spike and Management.

The spreadsheet can be found on Google Team Drives - Alaress -> Product Team -> Sprint Actuals

## What do I log time against?
Task | Logging in Sprint Actuals | Logging in Function Point
--- | --- | ---
Scrum meetings | Dev | Development on the next major release
Workshops | Spike | Development on the next major release
Any coding, reviewing, or QA'ing a custom work ticket | Dev | The job that is linked in the ticket
Any work on an Activity | Spike | Development on the next major release
Coding, reviewing, or QA'ing a Story or Task in the Sprint Forecast Lane | Dev | Development on the next major release
Coding, reviewing, or QA'ing a Story or Task in the Express Lane | BAU | Development on the next major release
Backlog grooming | Spike | Development on the current release
Coding, reviewing or QA'ing master bugs | BAU | Development on the current release
Coding, reviewing or QA'ing develop bugs | BAU | Development on the next major release
Preparing any meetings | Management | Development on the next major release
Non development work, or any away time | AFK | House - Internal
