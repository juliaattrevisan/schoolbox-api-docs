Resource Booking System

The resource booking system allows staff to reserve resources like class rooms and AV equipment. Before you begin you should be aware of the following. Each resource has a maintainer. Resources can be in one of the following three states; free for booking, booking following maintainer approval and maintainer bookings only. To book items that are maintainer bookings only you must contact the maintainer directly for approval. Bookings may be made on a timetable basis or at specific time depending on your requirements.

The AV department requests that you only book equipment for the time required and you promptly return the equipment after use.
