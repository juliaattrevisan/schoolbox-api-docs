Log in Assistance

The username for parent Lampada accounts is the full email address you nominated for regular communication with Shore; for example: if you nominated parent@mydomain.com.au as your email address, your Lampada username will be parent@mydomain.com.au

If you forget your password, enter your username in the box below and press the "Retrieve Password" button to receive an email containing your password. The email will be sent immediately, if you don't receive it promptly please check whether the message has been blocked by your anti-spam software or in your junk mail folder.

Parents are unable to change their password.

If after following this process you are still unable to login please send the detail of your difficulties to bursar@shore.nsw.edu.au.
