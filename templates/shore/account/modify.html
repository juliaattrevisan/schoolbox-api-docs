Before changing your Lampada password please note the following important information;

To minimise the risk of unauthorised access to your private information Shore School provided you with a Lampada password that was complex and therefore hard to guess or derive. The new password you choose must be at least 6 characters long and include at least one number, one letter and one symbol or punctuation character. When choosing a new password please;

  1. Use a string of letters and numbers that is familiar to you but not easily attributable to you. Avoid names, dictionary words and birthdates – unless you take steps to disguise them.
  2. Do not use a password that you are already using for another service.
  3. Do not use sequences of numbers or letters from rows on the keyboard.
  4. Take care with upper and lower case letters as they are considered to be different (eg. if you use the letter B in your password, you must type a capital B when you login, b will not work)

If you would rather keep your existing password but can’t remember it, use the forgotten password page to retrieve it.
