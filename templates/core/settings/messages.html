Message Settings

This page allows you to customise the settings for when and how you receive messages. Options that are unavailable have been disabled by your system administrator.

  • Messages sent via Email Digest are collated and emailed to you {DIGEST_FREQUENCY}. They will not appear in your internal messages list.
  • Messages sent via Instant will appear in your internal messages list.
  • Messages sent via Instant and Email will appear in your internal messages list, as well as be immediately emailed to you.
