Date Format special characters

d
Day of month, leading zero (01 to 31)
j
Day of month, no leading zero (1 to 31)
S
Suffix for day of month ("st" in "1st")
D
Day name, 3 letters (Mon to Sun)
l
Day name in full (Monday to Sunday)
M
Month name, 3 letters (Jan to Dec)
F
Month name in full (January to December)
m
Month number, leading zero (01 to 12)
n
Month number, no leading zero (1 to 12)
y
Year, 2 digits
Y
Year, 4 digits

A

H
24 hour time, leading zero (00 to 23)
G
24 hour time, no leading zero (0 to 23)
i
Minutes, leading zero (00 to 59)
s
Seconds, leading zero (00 to 59)
