# sbx design system

![a whale looking into a pantry](/whale-and-a-pantry.jpg)

## Overview

Within you'll find the **beginnings** of our design system for Schoolbox.
The ultimate goal is for this space to one day entirely replace our existing in product [**Pantry**](https://demo.schoolbox.com.au/pantry).
This new Design System will go beyond just being a collection of our existing UI scattered over a few pages.
Its ultimate aim is to be our single source of truth for Schoolbox's entire **Design System** in a much more comprehensive and well documented way than currently provided.

Once this is achieved, the Pantry will be converted into a space where Schools can more easily configure any available skin config options and see the changes in real time.
