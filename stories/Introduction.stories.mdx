## Overview

Welcome to the Schoolbox Pantry.
Within you'll find the **beginnings** of our design system for Schoolbox.
The ultimate goal is for The Pantry to one day entirely replace our existing in product [**Pantry**](https://demo.schoolbox.com.au/pantry).
This new Pantry will go beyond just being a collection of our existing UI scattered over a few pages.
Its ultimate aim is to be our single source of truth for Schoolbox's entire **Design System** in a much more comprehensive and well documented way than currently provided.
