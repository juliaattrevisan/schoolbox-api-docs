## Overview

Welcome to the Schoolbox Pantry.
Within you'll find our _in progress_ design system for Schoolbox products.
The ultimate goal is for The Pantry to one day replace our existing in product [**Pantry**](https://demo.schoolbox.com.au/pantry). Unlike previously, The Pantry is not only a collection of our existing UI components, but also our single source of truth for Schoolbox's entire design system in a much more comprehensive way than currently provided.
