Guidelines of Use and Privacy Policy

Shore School provides Lampada to support the learning, teaching and operational needs of the School and for communication between members of the Shore Community. Use of Lampada by Staff and Students is governed by School policy which is deemed to be accepted at login time. These guidelines are a summary of School policy that is primarily intended for Shore Parents, however they apply to anyone that has access to Lampada.

Regardless of your relationship with Shore School, Lampada must only be used to support the learning, teaching and operational activities of Shore School. Take care when adding information to Lampada, in particular make sure that what you add isn't offensive, threatening, defamatory, racist, discriminatory or illegal. Inappropriate use of Lampada may result in the loss of access to Lampada for the person or people responsible and in the event that the inappropriate use is illegal the matter will be referred to Police.

Staff, Students and Parents are issued with unique account credentials for Lampada, these are used to determine which information is accessible to the account holder. Don't use someone else's account to access Lampada. Any information that you add to Lampada will be widely available to other Lampada users unless you take steps to deliberately restrict access. For this reason it is best to assume that whatever you add is visible to all Lampada users.

Shore School continuously monitors the use of Lampada to ensure that use complies with the above guidelines. Any information that you contribute to Lampada is identifiable to you along with the date and time of the contribution. Individual Lampada pages may be inspected for compliance with the above guidelines at any time.

For information on how Shore School uses and manages personal information, refer to Shore School's Privacy Policy.
