Computer Acceptable Use Policy

The school has provided computers for use by students, offering access to a vast amount of information for use in your studies. The computers are provided and maintained for the benefit of all students. You are responsible for good behaviour with the resources and on the Internet just as you are in a classroom. Remember that access is a privilege, not a right and inappropriate use will result in that privilege being withdrawn.

You must know, understand and respect the laws relating to intellectual property, ownership of data, individual’s right to privacy and system security.
You must use the school network ethically and appropriately.

In using the school network (intranet and internet) I will:

  • Use a secure password
  • Use school resources as part of my school work and as directed by my teachers
  • Maintain privacy and confidentiality and respect the personal rights of others
  • NOT damage, disable, attempt to bypass or alter network settings, or otherwise harm the operation of the network
  • NOT share my UserID and password , even with my friends
  • NOT give personal information (mine or others) to anyone
  • NOT access, modify or destroy data, files, programs belonging to other users
  • NOT download or distribute copyrighted or illegal material
  • NOT use the network to offend, humiliate, intimidate or ridicule anyone
  • NOT knowingly transmit a computer virus onto the network

In addition, with online communication I will:

  • Use language appropriate for school
  • Only post data that I am comfortable everyone seeing (parents, teachers, friends)
  • Use constructive / productive / purposeful comments
  • NOT bully or provoke others with my comments
  • NOT post any information more personal than my first name
  • NOT post photos of myself or others without the teacher’s permission

If I receive or view material of a violent, dangerous, racist, or inappropriate content, I will report such messages to my teacher.

Electronic Communication between Staff, Students and Parents.

While after-school use of technology by students is the responsibility of parents, The Cathedral School policy requires that no students attending the school, or staff employed by the school, may identify, discuss, photograph or otherwise publish personal information or person opinions about school staff, fellow students or the school.

This covers in-school use and beyond-school use. Any breach of this will incur consequences as described in the School’s Behaviour Management Policy.

Any form of electronic communication (emailing, texting, social networking sites) initiated by staff to other staff, students or parents must be formal and professional, and only be used in relation to academic issues, organisational issues or health related issues.

Please read this document carefully.

Access to the network will be permitted only when you have agreed to these conditions.

If you violate these provisions, access to the Internet will be denied and you will be subject to disciplinary action.

I have read and understand the above and agree to use the school computer facilities within these guidelines.
